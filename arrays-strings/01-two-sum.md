## Problem: Two Sum (Easy)

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/two-sum/

### Approach

I used two nested loops to check every possible pair of numbers.
When the sum of two numbers equals the target, their indices are returned.

### Complexity

- Time: O(n²)
- Space: O(1)

### Notes

The solution should return the indices of the two numbers.
I also tested a case with duplicate values locally.
